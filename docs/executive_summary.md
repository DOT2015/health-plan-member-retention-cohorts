# Executive summary: new-member retention and onboarding outreach

**For:** VP of Member Experience (simulated scenario). **Data:** synthetic, 28,000 Medicare Advantage members enrolled Jan 2023-Jun 2025, observed through Dec 2025. No real members or organizations.

## The question

Should the plan fund a 90-day onboarding outreach team (welcome call, then health risk assessment, then first primary care visit)? If so, how many staff, and who should they call first?

## What we found

1. **12-month retention is 83.8%** across all cohorts (95% CI 83.3%-84.2%).
2. **Members who see a primary care doctor in their first 90 days stay longer.** Among members still enrolled at day 90, 12-month retention is 93.0% with a visit and 86.7% without. For dual-eligible members it is 88.3% vs 80.2%. After accounting for dual status, channel, plan type, and age, members with a visit leave at 0.53 times the rate of members without one (95% CI 0.49-0.58).
3. **This does not prove that the visit causes members to stay.** Members who book an early visit may be more engaged to begin with.
4. **Online enrollees fall behind at the health risk assessment.** 45.5% of online members who complete the welcome call finish the assessment, against about 60.4% in other channels.
5. **Departures run 2.12 times higher in January-March.** Month-end reports miss 2.0% of departures because some are posted 30-60 days late.

## What it would cost and return

- Outreach list: 769 new members a month without a visit by day 60 (January peak: 1,701). That is about 4.8 full-time staff, so 5 FTE at $325K a year (simulated cost).
- A 10-point rise in the visit rate would retain about 65 more members at month 12 if the link were fully causal, 32 if half causal, 16 if a quarter causal.
- Revenue over the first 12 months: $300K at best, below the staff cost. Over 24 months: $397K to $1.59M. One year of outreach pays for itself from 24 months of retained revenue if at least 20% of the link is causal.

## Recommendations

1. **Pilot before you fund.** Randomize outreach for one month of new members (about 234 per group) to confirm that calls raise the visit rate. A pilot cannot measure the retention effect directly; that would need 33,063 members per group, about 7 years of outreach volume.
2. **Fix the online assessment step.** Build the assessment into the online enrollment flow. This is the cheapest gap to close.
3. **Call dual-eligible members first and staff for January.** Duals without an early visit have the lowest retention (80.2%). Report churn by effective date and restate prior months when late terminations post.
